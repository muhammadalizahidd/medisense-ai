04 · Accent system

15 presets, two families, one rule

Each preset sets accent · accent-fill · fill-hover · fill-pressed · accent-tint · on-accent for light and dark themes. On-accent text is picked automatically: white when it reaches 4.5:1, otherwise ink #1A1406. Blue, Green, Evergreen, Olive and Bronze are your current palette colours; their fills are exact, and the text shade is darkened slightly where needed for 4.5:1 on every theme.

Custom colour: generation

  1. Take the picked hex into OKLCH. Keep hue, clamp chroma to 0.04–0.20.
  2. Fill: lightness stepped until white reaches 4.5:1; if no step works within L 0.45–0.70, use ink on-accent instead.
  3. Hover / pressed: fill L −0.05 / −0.10 (light), +0.05 / −0.06 (dark).
  4. Text: darkened (light) or lightened (dark) until ≥ 4.5:1 on page, surface and sunken of the active theme.
  5. Tint: L 0.96 C 0.03 (light); L 0.26 C 0.05 (dark).

Warnings before saving

Close to “At Risk” red. Hue within 18° of a semantic colour. Status tags will still read, because they always carry a label, but buttons may look like warnings.
Fails contrast on Midnight. Text 3.8:1, needs 4.5:1. We adjusted it to #9DB4FF; keep the adjustment or pick another colour.

Red, Amber, Green and Emerald presets show the same “close to” note in the picker but remain allowed.
