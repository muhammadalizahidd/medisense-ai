Principles

01

Systems supporting therapists

AI output is always a draft on a draft surface. Nothing a model wrote looks finished until a person signs it.

02

Three layers, never mixed

AI drafts (dashed edge), rules compute (solid edge, “Rule” or “Automation” tag), people decide (signature: name, time, lock).

03

Calm confidence

Pressure comes with its reason. At Risk is a labelled tag and a “why” line, never a flashing banner.

04

Density with breathing room

40px rows, 14px body, hairline separation. Dense tables sit inside generous 40px page gutters.

05

Colour means something

Neutral by default. The accent marks the primary action and selection; semantic colours mark status and never take the accent.

06

One of everything

One shell, one header (two sizes), one panel, one table, one tag system, one sheet. Live and sample modules share them.

The three-layer language

One mark per layer, the same on every screen. The AI mark replaces the sparkle and wand icons: a small hollow diamond on a dashed draft surface.

How the 12 observed problems are resolved

#ProblemDecision
1Hero too tallCompact header (≈104px: back link, serif title 32, meta row, tags, actions) on every working page. Landing hero only on Overview and therapist home.
2Inconsistent languageFlat and editorial: no gradients, no glow, no resting shadows. The landing hero is a flat surface panel with a large serif greeting.
3Duplicate-looking modules“Live care” pinned first with a live dot. Sample modules carry a quiet “Sample data” tag in the header and sidebar group. Routes unchanged; groups collapse.
4Long recordsOne Tabs component: underline style, counts, alert count for waiting items, sticky under the top bar, kept in the URL.
5Tables overflowColumns carry a priority (P1–P3). Below 1024 P3 hides; below 768 rows stack into cards showing P1 plus the status tag.
6Noisy statusPrimary status = tinted tag with dot. Secondary meta (Telehealth, Invoiced, Patient confirmed) = outline or plain icon + text, never tinted.
7Selection patternsSegmented = filter a view (≤6). Choice pills = one answer in a form (≤7). Select = long lists. Tick = declarations and consents. Switch = on/off settings.
8AI / rules / peopleThe three-layer language above, on every screen.
9Thin feedbackToasts after decisions, inline “Saved” with time, field errors under the field plus a summary at the top of the sheet.
10Patient app in a frameDesigned at 390×844 first; the desktop frame only wraps it. Safe areas and bottom sheets are part of the app, not the frame.
11No dark mode13 themes (your 6 palettes + 7 new, 3 dark), 15 accents + custom, 6 typefaces, Match system, density and text size.
12Serif at small sizesSerif tokens start at 24px. Panel titles, tabs and labels are always Instrument Sans.
